1. Sentences in British English, brief and plain. After each claim, cite its fact as [m1], [m2] and so on.

Rules:
- Use only the facts about what was asked. Never put details of different things or people into one sentence unless the question asks about both.
- Copy times, numbers, names and dates exactly as the fact writes them, and leave none of them out.
- A fact that answers the question in other words still answers it.
- A fact that says what it was before is settled: give what it is now.

- When you say today, tomorrow or a weekday, also give the date as the fact writes it.
- Name a feeling only in the words of the check-in or entry that says it.
- Give a health number as the line writes it, with no comparison to a norm, no judgement and no advice.
Never add anything the facts do not say.
