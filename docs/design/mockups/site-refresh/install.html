Get Nocturne.

Free and open source, for Mac, Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone. No account, nothing to sign in to.

Choose your computer or phone below. Each link gives the latest version.

Mac

Apple chip, M1 or later · macOS 26 or later

For this computer

Download for Mac

Nocturne-macos-arm64.dmg

Macs with an Intel chip are not supported. To see which chip yours has, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac.

The first time you open it

  1. Open the downloaded file, then drag Nocturne into Applications.
  2. Open Nocturne from Applications. The first time, your Mac asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click Open.

Nocturne for Mac is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

For this computer

Download for Windows

Nocturne-windows-x64-setup.exe

Installs for you alone and needs no administrator password. Computers managed by an organisation can use the .msi installer instead.

The first time you open it

  1. Your browser may warn that the file is not commonly downloaded. In Edge, open the … menu beside the file, choose Keep, then Show more, then Keep anyway.
  2. Open the installer. Windows shows Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

You only do this once. Microsoft charges a yearly fee to sign an app so it opens without asking; Nocturne is free and open source, and this release is not signed yet.

64-bit computer with an Intel, AMD or Arm chip

For this computer

Other computers and phones

To make sure a download is the one we published, compare it with the fingerprints in SHA256SUMS. The install guide shows how, explains each package and how to take Nocturne off again. Earlier versions are on the releases page.

What your computer needs.

Almost certainly, if it has 8 GB of memory: an 8 GB MacBook Air, or a typical 8 GB Windows or Linux laptop.

Memory
8 GB is enough. On an 8 GB computer Nocturne uses about 5 GB at its busiest and gives it back when it has nothing to do. With more memory it offers a larger setup with clearer answers.
A graphics card
No graphics card needed. Macs with Apple silicon, and computers with AMD or Intel graphics built in, run Nocturne on the chip and share the computer’s memory. An AMD or NVIDIA graphics card works too, best with 6 GB or more. Other computers run it on the processor.
An AI chip
Helpful, never needed. On AMD Ryzen AI 300 series computers and newer, with 16 GB or more, the AI chip reads your voice notes. On many with 32 GB or more it also reads what you keep, and on some it answers your questions too, so the rest of the computer has less to do.
The system
macOS 26 or later on an Apple chip, Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or 64-bit Linux on Intel, AMD or Arm.
A phone
Android 8 or later, or an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The phone app works on its own, or with Nocturne on your computer.

Downloads at setup are between about 3 GB and 26 GB, depending on the setup, and about 9 GB more where the AI chip reads what you keep. After that, Nocturne works with no internet at all.

After you install.

On a computer, Nocturne opens a short setup. You can keep things straight away.

  1. When it opens

    It looks at the computer

    It tells you which setup it will use. You can always pick a smaller one, which uses less memory, space and battery.

  2. Once, when you agree

    It downloads what it needs

    It asks first, then downloads what this computer can run: between about 3 GB and 26 GB, and about 9 GB more where the AI chip reads what you keep. This is the only time Nocturne needs the internet, and it carries on by itself if the connection drops.

  3. At the time you choose

    Your first page

    From then on, Nocturne works with no internet at all.
