About the backup

  • WhereIn your iCloud, in a place only Nocturne uses. It doesn’t show among your files.In your Google Drive, in a folder called Nocturne backup. Nocturne can reach only what it puts there, nothing else in your Drive.
  • WhenEach night while this phone charges, at a time your phone chooses. Over mobile data too, unless you choose Wi-Fi only.
  • How manyYou can go back up to seven nights. When you forget something for good, it goes from the backup at once.
  • Private thingsThings you mark private are backed up too, locked like everything else. Private only keeps them out of notifications, reminders and the lock screen.
  • A new phoneSign in with the same Apple Account, install Nocturne, choose I have a backup, and type your phrase.Install Nocturne, choose I have a backup, choose the same Google account, and type your phrase.
