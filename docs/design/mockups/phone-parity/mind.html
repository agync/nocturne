Nocturne’s mind online · step 4 of 4

Before you turn this on, here is exactly what happens.

While this is on, what you keep and your questions go from this phone to answers.example, so it can read them, write your page and answer.

What is sent

Each thing you keep, so it can be read: its words, and the photo or recording itself when there is one. Your journal entries, good things and check-ins, so it can write your reflections and recaps. Each question, with the lines that answer it. Each night, the lines your page is written from. For example:

Kept 7:40 am · Dentist moved to Thursday at 9, Bridge Street.

Where it goes

This phone sends it straight to answers.example, which reads it and writes back. Nocturne keeps what it learned on this phone, with its receipts, and checks every receipt before you see it. Nothing passes through Nocturne’s makers.

Who can see it

answers.example can see everything sent, when it is sent, and this phone’s internet address.

Private

Marking something private keeps it from coming up on its own, in reminders and notifications. It still goes to be read and helps answer what you ask. Things you let go are never sent.
