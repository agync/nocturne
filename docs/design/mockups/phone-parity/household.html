What Tom can see

Tom can see

  • This phone’s name
  • That it borrows Hallway’s mind
  • When it last used it, and how much: how many things were read, how many questions, and the mind’s time

Tom can’t see, in Nocturne

  • What you keep, ask or write
  • Your answers, your page or your journal
  • Your calendar and health

Hallway reads each thing in its memory while it works on it, then lets it go. It keeps nothing, and no record of what it read. Things you marked private never go to Hallway. No one else in the household sees anything of yours, and you see nothing of theirs.

Someone with full control of Hallway could change Nocturne on it to look at what it reads. Nocturne can’t prevent that. Join only a household you trust.
