Rules for every form
  • No red, amber or green, no arrows, no targets, goals, scores or rings.
  • No “normal”, reference ranges or categories, including for blood pressure.
  • Every number and every dot is a receipt that opens its readings.
  • Your usual is the middle half of your own last 60 days, after two weeks of readings.
  • Every chart has a sentence and “Show as a list”.
  • Nocturne can’t tell what your numbers mean for your health. A doctor or nurse can.
