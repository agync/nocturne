Bring health readings to your computer?

  • Nocturne reads the kinds you choose from Health ConnectApple Health: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, blood pressure, steps and workouts. From the last 30 days, then each new reading.
  • Only to your computer, in locked messages only your computer can open. Nocturne has no account and keeps nothing anywhere else.
  • Your journal shows how you slept and moved beside what you wrote, and your page can show last night’s sleep.
  • Nocturne never adds or changes anything in Health. It can’t tell what your numbers mean for your health. A doctor or nurse can.

How Nocturne uses your health readings

  • WhatNocturne reads only the kinds you allow: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, blood pressure, steps, workouts and, if you turn it on, weight. It never writes to Health ConnectHealth.
  • WhyTo show your readings beside your journal and last night’s sleep on your morning page, on your own computer.
  • WhereReadings go only to the computer you paired, in locked messages only it can open. Nobody else receives them, including the people who make Nocturne. Nocturne has no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • Keeping and stoppingYour computer keeps readings until you delete them there. Turn Health off in Nocturne’s This phone, or take the permission back in Health Connectthe Health app. To delete what went across, use From your phones on your computer.
