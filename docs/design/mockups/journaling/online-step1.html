Online answers · step 1 of 5

Have your answers written by an online service you choose.

Sent
Your question, with a few short lines from what you kept that help answer it, your journal entries and good things included.
Never sent
Your photos, recordings and documents themselves, your check-ins, reflections and recaps, and anything you marked private.
You need
An account with that service. Most charge a little for each question. Setting up means copying three details from its website.
What is sent
Your question, and the few things you kept that answer it, as short sentences. A line from a journal entry or a good thing can go too, when it helps answer the question. For example, if you ask about your eye test, this is all that leaves:
Your question when is my eye test? What you kept that answers it
  1. 1The eye test with Dr Patel is on Friday 20 November at 11:20 in room 12. (kept Monday 9 November)
  2. 2Dr Patel will send the eye test results by 27 November. (kept Monday 9 November)
And Nocturne’s standard instructions, the same for everyone: answer only from these lines, and say which line each part came from. Not sent: the clinic’s letter, your voice note, your name, or anything else you kept.
Never sent
Photos, recordings and documents stay here. Things you marked private are never sent. Your check-ins, reflections and recaps are never sent: they are written and kept on this computer. Nocturne also keeps back lines that look like health, money or ID details unless you choose otherwise. It can miss some, so mark anything you never want sent as private. Journal entries and good things can’t be marked private, so while this is on, a line from one can go with a question it helps answer.
Who sees it
The service you choose can see each question and what goes with it. Every answer says it was written there.
What changes
Your questions are answered online by the service you set up. Answers may take a few seconds longer.
What it costs
Most services charge a little for each question, on your account with them. You choose how many questions a month may go online.
What you need
An account with an online service. Its details are added under Advanced details, where Nocturne checks that they work.

Stays on this computer

  • Finding what answers your question
  • Keeping, reading and listening
  • The overnight tidy and your page
  • Checking where each answer came from
  • Writing your reflections and recaps

If the service can’t be reached, this computer answers, and says so.

Turn it off at any time, in Settings or on the Privacy page.

Nothing is turned on until the last step.
