Online answers · step 5 of 5

Before you turn this on, here is exactly what happens.

While this is on, each question and the few lines that answer it leave this computer. Everything else you kept stays here.

What is sent, for each question

Your question, and the few things you kept that answer it, as short sentences, lines from your journal entries and good things included. At most 12, each with the day you kept it. For example:

Your question when is my eye test? What you kept that answers it
  1. 1The eye test with Dr Patel is on Friday 20 November at 11:20 in room 12. (kept Monday 9 November)
  2. 2Dr Patel will send the eye test results by 27 November. (kept Monday 9 November)
And Nocturne’s standard instructions, the same for everyone.

Nocturne’s engine on this computer sends each question straight to that service, with the details you gave. Nothing passes through Nocturne’s makers: there is nothing in between for them to run.

Who can see it

api.example.com can see each question, the lines sent with it, when you ask, and this computer’s internet address. What it uses to write answers, example-model, may be made by another company that the service passes questions to.

Nocturne can’t check what the service keeps, for how long, or whether it learns from your questions. Read its privacy page before you turn this on.

Never sent

  • Photos, recordings and documents, including the ones these lines came from
  • Things you marked private
  • Your check-ins, reflections and recaps. They are written on this computer and stay here.
  • Everything else you kept, your pages and your name

Nocturne doesn’t guess what is private. Mark anything you never want sent as private.

Journal entries and good things can’t be marked private, so while this is on, a line from one can go with a question it helps answer.

What changes

Your questions are answered by api.example.com, on your account with them, so it may charge for each one. Answers may take a few seconds longer. If it can’t be reached, this computer answers, and says so.

Changing your mind

There is no limit on how many questions go online. Turn it off at any time in Settings or on the Privacy page. Every answer says where it was written, and “See what was sent” shows the exact words.
