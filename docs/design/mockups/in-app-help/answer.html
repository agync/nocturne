You asked

“How do I get Nocturne on my phone?”

From the Nocturne guide

Away from home lets your phone ask Nocturne a question, keep something and read today’s page while you are out. It is off until you turn it on.

Turn it on

  1. Open Away from home and turn it on.
  2. Choose a connection.
  3. Nocturne asks before it downloads the small helper program the connection needs.
  4. Your computer must stay on and awake for the phone to reach it.

Add a phone

  1. Choose Add a phone.
  2. On the phone, scan the code with its camera.
  3. Name the phone.
  4. Compare the three words.
