Who can see it

answers.example can see everything sent, when it is sent, and this phone’s internet address. What it uses, reader-small, may be made by another company that the service passes things to.

Nocturne can’t check what the service keeps, for how long, or whether it learns from what you send. Read its privacy page before you turn this on.

Private

Marking something private keeps it from coming up on its own, in reminders and notifications. It still goes to be read and helps answer what you ask. Things you let go are never sent.

What changes

answers.example reads what you keep, writes your page each morning and answers your questions, on your account with them, so it may charge for each. Reading happens when this phone allows it, often while it charges. If it can’t be reached, what you keep waits to be read and Ask finds by words.
