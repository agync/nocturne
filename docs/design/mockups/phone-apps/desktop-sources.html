Add a phone

Scan it with the Nocturne app on your phone. No app? Your phone’s camera opens Nocturne in its browser instead.

Can’t scan? In the app, choose Type the code, then type:

The four wordsamber otter quiet river

The codeK7QM2A X9DPLR 4TWB8N H3VC6E

No app? In your phone’s browser, open amber-otter-quiet-river.trycloudflare.com and type the code.

The code lasts five minutes and works once.

Get the free Nocturne app: search for “Nocturne” in Google Play or the App Store. It has the same half-moon mark.
