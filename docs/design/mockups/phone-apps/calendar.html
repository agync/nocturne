Bring your calendar to your computer?

  • Nocturne reads what’s in this phone’s calendar: each appointment’s title, time, place and who’s invited, from two days ago to two weeks ahead.
  • It goes only to your computer, the same way as everything this phone keeps: in locked messages only your computer can open. Nocturne has no account and keeps nothing anywhere else.
  • Your page shows what’s on today and this week, and Ask can answer “When is the dentist?” Each line shows which calendar it came from.
  • Nocturne never adds, changes or deletes anything in your calendar.
