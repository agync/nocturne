[Journaling](/design/journaling.md) proposes a Journal entry in the rail: a daily entry in words,
voice and photos, the day's timeline of everything kept, a feelings check-in adapted from mood
meter research, up to three good things a day, people and places shown on each entry to confirm,
correct or remove, a reflection on any day at any time, and week, month and year recaps where
every line cites its entry. Entries never fade, and looking back does not count as use.

The owner answered every open question, and the page records the decisions. The journal has no
private switch: its content is never in a notification, the tray or anything read aloud, and is
shown in full everywhere else, including on a paired phone, which reads the whole journal. A
table says what counts as a journal item, and a proposed third sensitivity, `journal`, carries the
rule to memories read from entries and good things; check-ins yield mentions only. With Online
answers on, Ask may send the entry and good-thing sentences a question needs; reflections, recaps
and check-ins never go, and the Online answers setup and settings say so, including that journal
entries can't be marked private. A section lists the changes other pages need when it is built.
Mockups for the computer and the phone, in light and dark, and copies of the Online answers steps
with the new words, are in `docs/design/mockups/journaling/`. None of it is in the interface yet.
