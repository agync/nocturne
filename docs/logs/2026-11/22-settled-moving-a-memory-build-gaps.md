[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md) gives the computer's lines before and after it
restarts to open a moved memory, which [decision 31](/design/phone-parity.md#decisions) records as
decided by build cost for the owner to confirm. The phone puts its memory aside only once the
computer says the memory opened. A computer short of room says so before and during a move, a move
can be stopped on the computer, and "Stop moving?" backs out with "Keep moving". The time estimate
waits until the move's speed is measured. When a computer's memory is replaced, its other phones
send their calendar and health again. Each thing carries the name of the device it was kept on.
