[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md#when-the-computer-already-holds-a-memory) follows the
owner's decision: a computer that already holds a memory asks how a phone alone joins it. "Bring the
phone's things in" adds the phone's kept things, journal entries and check-ins to the computer's
memory, which reads them as new, and the phone sends its calendar and health again. "Use this
computer's memory" brings nothing. Either way the phone becomes a companion and its own memory is
put aside on it, kept until removed when nothing of it reached the computer. Putting the computer's
memory aside stays only in restoring a backup file. [Decision 32](/design/phone-parity.md#decisions)
records it, and the computer's mockups gain the choice and the result.
