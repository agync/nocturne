[Phone and computer as equal starting points](/design/phone-parity.md) proposes a ladder from a
phone alone, to a locked copy online and an online mind, to a household computer many phones join,
to a computer of one's own with companion phones, with personas, journeys, what each mode shows,
who sees what, the rule and decision record changes it needs, phasing, risks and decisions for the
owner. [Nocturne on a phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md) covers first run without a
computer, Today with no mind, the locked copy and choosing a mind. [A household
computer](/design/household-server.md) covers joining by borrowing its mind or keeping a memory
there, exactly what the person who runs it can see, and the household screen on the computer.
[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md) covers one home at a time. Mockups for Android,
iPhone and the computer, light and dark, are in `docs/design/mockups/phone-parity/`.
