On Android, the permission for readings older than 30 days is now asked for with the kinds
wherever Health Connect offers it, so a phone restored from a backup reads its readings again that
far back. "Also bring older readings" still decides how far a first read goes otherwise. See
[Health on the phone](/design/phone-health.md).
