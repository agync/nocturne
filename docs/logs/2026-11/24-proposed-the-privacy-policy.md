[Privacy policy](/design/privacy-policy.md) is new. It holds the text of the page at `/privacy/`,
effective 24 November 2026: what Nocturne keeps, each time something leaves a device and where it
goes, the phone's permissions, health information, Google user data, deleting and exporting, and
the website. Each claim is mapped to the code or design that shows it. The nightly backup's
sections wait until it ships, and raw health readings are left out of that backup, as the owner
decided today. Seven questions wait for the owner.
