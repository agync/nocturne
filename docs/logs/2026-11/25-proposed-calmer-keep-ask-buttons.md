[The buttons in the keep or ask bar](/design/keep-ask-buttons.md) measures why the two round
buttons look stuck on: 1 px of room above and below them, four outlines in one row, three corner
styles and a stray-looking "or" beside the bar. It shows two options with before and after captures
in both themes, a narrow window and 200% text, and recommends quiet icons. None of it is in the
interface yet.
