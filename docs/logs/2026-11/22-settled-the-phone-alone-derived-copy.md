[Nocturne on a phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md) settles the strings the build had to
derive. Deleting what the calendar or Health brought keeps the computer's "Also stop" tick, on by
default. A put aside memory has its own page, and two from one day show their times. The restore
card shows no date, since the only date readable before the passphrase is the file's own. An item
shows how it was kept, and "Forget…" shows only when Nocturne worked something out from it. The
private mark says it moves with the memory. This phone's help says how to take back the phone's
permissions, and Welcome points to it after Delete everything.
