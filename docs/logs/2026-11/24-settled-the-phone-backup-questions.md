The owner settled the five open questions of [Backup on a phone of its own](/design/phone-backup.md#decisions),
each as recommended.

- A recovery key can be made later in This phone with "Make a recovery key…". It replaces any earlier
  key and shows the new one once. The page gains its copy and two mockup states.
- A backup file restored onto a phone that has a memory brings its things in, as a backup from the
  account does, from the release that ships the nightly backup.
- Delete everything while offline deletes the phone at once and the backup when next online.
- A stopped backup shows once on Today, with no notification.
- A restore that brought things in has no undo.

The page's open questions are now its decisions.
