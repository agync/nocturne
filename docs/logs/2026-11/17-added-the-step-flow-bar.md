[Keeping the next step in view](/design/step-flow-actions.md) is built. Online answers, Away from
home, first-run setup, Make a backup and Restore keep their buttons in one bar pinned to the bottom
of the window, the main button first on the left, with the reason when Continue is off. Step 1 of
Online answers is three short lines and one expander, and step 5 says what changes. Step 1 of Away
from home is lighter in the same way, and step 2 now always comes before anything is downloaded or
turned on. The keep or ask bar is hidden below 720 pixels wide while a step flow is open.
