When a deletion of the backup is owed to an account the phone no longer reaches, This phone's hint
and Welcome's line after Delete everything say it goes from the iCloud or Google Drive it was in,
the next time Nocturne reaches it. The copy is new and waits for the owner's review. See
[Turn off](/design/phone-backup.md#turn-off).
