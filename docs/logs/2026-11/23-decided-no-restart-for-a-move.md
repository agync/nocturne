[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md#on-the-computer-a-phone-with-its-own-memory) no
longer restarts Nocturne on the computer. An empty computer brings the phone's things in without
asking, with the bringing-in stages on both sides, so the restart, finishing and arrived lines are
gone. Decision 31 in [Phone and computer as equal starting points](/design/phone-parity.md#decisions)
records the owner's decision of 2026-11-23: the end-to-end test showed a restart strands the phone
when a Simple connection's address changes. [Phone first](/design/phone-first.md) follows.
