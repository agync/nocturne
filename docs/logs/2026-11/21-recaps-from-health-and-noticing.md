A recap now has "From Health" after "How it felt": the nights as a strip for a week or a month,
steps by day, resting heart rate against the person's usual, and the workouts counted ("2 walks")
with each one a receipt. Blood pressure, heart rate and weight are not in recaps.

Under "What tended to go together", at most three sentences say what sleep, steps or workouts went
with: a person, place or thing written about, a part of the feelings field, or a good thing. A
sentence is shown only with at least three days with the thing and three without, a difference of
at least 30 minutes of sleep or 2,000 steps, and the pattern holding on at least three of the days.
For workouts, a part of the feelings field must be felt on a share of workout days at least 25
percentage points larger than its share of the days without one. A period still going leaves out
today. It says what went together, never
why, and it cites each night or day counted and each journal item that named the thing
([Health from the phone](/design/phone-health.md), decisions H3 and H4).

Neither is stored with the recap. Both are worked out from what is kept each time the recap is
opened, so a reading removed in Health or deleted on the computer is gone from them at once, and
"Notice what goes together" turns the sentences off. The delete preview counts the sentences that
deleting readings would change.
