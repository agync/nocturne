[Help with Nocturne, from the ask box](/design/in-app-help.md) records the owner's choice of flow:
a confident match is answered on this computer from the guide and what you kept, with guide
receipts, and an unsure one asks "Did you mean help with Nocturne?". It adds the guide reader to
Settings, rewords "answers only from what you kept" wherever it is claimed, keeps help off paired
phones, and sets an evaluation gate before each size of computer writes help. Mockups and before
and after renders are in `docs/design/mockups/in-app-help/`. None of it is in the interface yet.
