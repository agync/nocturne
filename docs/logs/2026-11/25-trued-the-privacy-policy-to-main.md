[Privacy policy](/design/privacy-policy.md) was checked against `main` again. Its public text now
says that a question carries today's date, time and time zone, that check-ins and journal lines
can go with it, and when a health reading can: on the computer when a question names a time and a
kind of reading, and on a phone alone also when the provider looks further. Reflections and recaps
still never go. Private things go with the computer's own questions and stay for a paired phone's.
The provider's list is looked at when the person chooses from it, Read now reads on battery, and
the nightly backup and the Google user data section are in the text, with the permissions table
to match. The Evidence table names files and functions, open question 4 is settled, and a new
question weighs the stores' rules on health readings sent to a provider the person chose.
