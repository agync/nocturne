The day's reflection may now say what the calendar held that day, as background and in a short
clause with its time: "..., and your calendar had the dentist at 9:00." It never says the person went,
because a calendar says what was planned. Every calendar counts alike, a cancelled entry is left
out, and each clause cites its calendar entry. A day with only appointments and nothing kept has
nothing to look back on, and the footer counts only what was kept. [Journaling](/design/journaling.md)
has the wording.
