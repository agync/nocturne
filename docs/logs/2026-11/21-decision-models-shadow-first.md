[Decision 0016](/adr/0016-decision-models-shadow-first.md) records how Nocturne starts on decision
models: typed questions asked beside today's rules and word lists, logged and never acting. The
first decider is the mind already loaded; the model this computer's hardware would use is named by
the fit logic and runs only in the decision check of the [evaluation](/design/evaluation.md).
Nothing a person sees changes, and nothing is downloaded for people.
