tags: [log, update]

The owner deferred two pages for one day. When a restore brings a backup into a phone that already
has a page or reflection for a day the backup has one for too, the phone's stays and the backup's
doesn't come. [Backup on a phone of its own](/design/phone-backup.md#decisions) records it as
decision 6, and the "From your backup" mark is gone from the design.
