[Backup on a phone of its own](/design/phone-backup.md) is new and holds the whole backup design
that [Nocturne on a phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md#backup) now summarises: three steps to
turn it on, with the phrase typed back and Wi-Fi only before the first backup; a recovery key
offered once; This phone with its waiting and stopped states; turning it off and what happens to
the copies; restoring onto an empty phone; bringing a backup's things into a phone that already
holds a memory, worded as a computer bringing a phone's things in; and how forgetting, Delete
everything and private reach the backup. The mockups gain the new states, a restore file and
renders at 200 per cent text.
