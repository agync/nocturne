[The journal and the other capabilities on the site](/design/site-capabilities.md) assesses what
the landing page promises today, which promises the journal and the capabilities designed since
strengthen or strain, and what should stay off the site. It proposes one new band, "Your day, in
your own words.", between How it works and Privacy, rewords two privacy promises so Online answers
is described honestly now that journal lines can go with a question, and recaptures the phone
picture with the Journal tile. Mockups show each changed band before and after, in both themes, at
desktop and phone widths. It lists 17 new or changed strings per language and depends on the
journal shipping first. None of it is on the site yet.

A copy pass then brought the band and the two privacy details into the site's voice: shorter
promises, nothing the hero already says, and Online answers described in plain words.
