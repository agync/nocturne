[A household computer](/design/household-server.md) keeps no activity line about a borrowing phone,
stops only borrowing phones when lending stops, lets a removed person take their memory home, and
shows each phase's screens only once it ships. [Nocturne on a phone of its
own](/design/phone-first.md) adds an item's page with its private mark, the phone's Delete
everything words and the computer's part of the first release. [Nocturne on Android and
iPhone](/design/phone-apps.md) starts every calendar on, by the rule against judging data.
