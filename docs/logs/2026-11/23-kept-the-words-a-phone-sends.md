The owner decided that the Sent online list in [Nocturne on a phone of its
own](/design/phone-first.md) keeps the exact words of each request, as 0011's list does on the
computer. Each row names its host and opens "Exactly what was sent", with a photo or a recording
named rather than shown. When something a row carried is forgotten, its words go at once and the
row stays, marked "Removed when you forgot it". Delete everything removes the whole list.
