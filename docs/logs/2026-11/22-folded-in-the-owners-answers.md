[A household computer](/design/household-server.md) keeps nothing about phones that borrow its mind:
one household code, shown as a QR code, admits any phone holding it, and "New code" cuts off every
phone holding the old one, with no per-phone pause or removal. The owner's companion phone can show
the code, make a new one and turn lending on or off. Check-ins go, sealed, to a borrowed household
mind. [Phone and computer as equal starting points](/design/phone-parity.md) records decisions 22 to
30 and treats a phone alone with an online mind as complete, with no copy nudging towards a computer
or a household. [Nocturne on a phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md) makes the locked copy daily
by default, with weekly and monthly as choices. Mockups for the household and the locked copy are
redrawn.
