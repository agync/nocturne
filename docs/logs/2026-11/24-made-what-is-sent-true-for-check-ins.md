With [Ask with tools](/design/ask-with-tools.md) built, a check-in or a health reading can go with a
question it helps answer, Online answers on the computer included. The setup and settings words,
the check-in's own note, the phone's Health screen, the [Online answers guide](/guide/online-answers.md),
[Journaling](/design/journaling.md), the [data model](/design/data-model.md) and decisions
[0011](/adr/0011-online-answers.md) and [0015](/adr/0015-journal-privacy-class.md) now say so.
Reflections, recaps and anything marked private are unchanged. The design page lists each string
before and after.
