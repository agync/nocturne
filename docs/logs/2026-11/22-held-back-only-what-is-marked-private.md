[Only what you mark private is held back](/design/only-marked-private.md) records the owner's rule
of 2026-11-22: Nocturne never judges a person's data. Online answers sends any memory that answers
a question unless it is marked private, being forgotten or let go, and its settings and setup lose
the "Health, money and ID details" switch. What Nocturne remembers no longer suggests marking a
memory private, and neither of the morning page's questions skips a memory for what it is about,
health, money, ID or medicine. One sentence, "Nocturne doesn’t guess what is private. Mark anything you never
want sent as private.", takes the old line's place. People who turned Online answers on before
the change see a one-time notice before their next question goes online, in Ask, on a paired phone
or in Online answers in Settings. [Keeping the next step in view](/design/step-flow-actions.md),
[Journaling](/design/journaling.md), [Journaling follow-ups](/design/journaling-follow-ups.md), the
[data model](/design/data-model.md), the [evaluation](/design/evaluation.md) and the Online answers
mockups follow.
