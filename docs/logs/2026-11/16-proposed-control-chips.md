[Control chips and easier reading in setup](/design/control-chips.md) answers a tester with
dyslexia who could not read the Tiny box on step 1 of setup. It proposes new words for that box,
a chip that shows a place or control with its own icon and words without being clickable, a gear
beside Settings in the rail, a chevron on "Why this one?", an inventory of the strings that point
at a place or control, and reading rules for setup that a test can check. Mockups in light and dark
show the box before and after. None of it is in the interface yet.
