description: "The Android app lists calendars from the work profile beside the personal ones, when Android lets the app see them."

status: stable
---

On Android 10 and newer, the calendar list in the phone app includes the work profile's calendars
when the phone lets the app see them, which depends on the device's administrator. They sit among
the others, start on like every other calendar, and their appointments flow the same way. Android
gives no app the names of a work profile's calendars, so each is named for where it comes from:
"Work profile calendar", or "Work profile calendar 2" and so on when there are several, with its
colour dot. Their appointments come without notes and without who is invited, because Android
shares neither, so the notes switch does not apply to them. When the phone does not share them, the
list is as it was and no message appears. [Phone apps](/design/phone-apps.md) has the rows.
