Decided 2026-11-22. When the phone app brings calendars to the computer, every calendar the phone
exposes starts on, holiday and birthday calendars too, and the person can turn any of them off.
The note under the list that explained the old default is gone, and the phone no longer tells the
computer what kind a calendar is. [Phone apps](/design/phone-apps.md) records the decision.
