[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md#when-the-computer-already-holds-a-memory) closes
the review of the build. What the person forgot on the phone stays forgotten on the computer, calendar
entries the phone sends again included, and memories marked private, done or kept forever there
keep those marks. After things are brought in, keeping what was put aside is selected, and a line
before removing lists what stays only on the phone: pages, reflections, answers to the morning
question, names taught, what was confirmed or settled, and settings. While any of those stay, what
was put aside never goes by itself (AGENTS.md invariant 5). The whole move and its restart are retired on both
sides; a computer old enough to take the memory whole gets the newer-Nocturne words. The room the
computer needs is about three times the phone's estimate of its backup.
