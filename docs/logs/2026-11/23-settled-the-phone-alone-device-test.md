[Nocturne on a phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md) settles the gaps a test on a real phone
found. This phone gains "Restore a backup…", so a phone that holds a memory reaches the replace
choice. On Android, Back from Journal or Ask goes to Today. After Delete everything, Welcome names
only the permissions the phone still grants, or says nothing. A phone alone's day runs from 4 am,
as in the journal, with "after midnight" on Today until then. Backups, checks and restores show
0013's determinate progress. Times follow the phone's 12 or 24-hour setting, and a voice note's
title is its first words, never a file name. The check-in saved screen and the empty journal day
lose their computer words.
