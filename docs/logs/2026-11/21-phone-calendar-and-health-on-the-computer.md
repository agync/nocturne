A paired phone app can now send its calendar and health readings through Away from home. The
computer keeps each entry or reading once, as a new version when it changes and marked removed when
it goes on the phone, and never deletes one silently. Appointments become one memory each and show
in "In your calendar today" and on the journal's day; last night's sleep shows under the page's
title and is never read aloud. From your phones lists what each phone brings, and its source can be
stopped or its data deleted through the usual preview. The export holds one calendar file per phone
and one table per kind of reading. The [data model](/design/data-model.md) and
[remote access](/architecture/remote.md) describe the storage and the two sends.
