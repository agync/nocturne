[Nocturne on Android and iPhone](/design/phone-apps.md) proposes native apps that replace the
phone's browser page: install from the store, pair by scanning or typing the code inside the app,
a bottom bar with Today, Journal and Ask, Keep one tap away and in every app's share menu, plain
asks for the camera, microphone, calendar and health with a working no, and states for a computer
that is away or on another version. The phone's calendar shows on the morning page and the
journal's timeline with receipts, and the computer gains From your phones to see, stop and delete
what phones bring. [Health from the phone](/design/phone-health.md) proposes sleep, heart rate,
resting heart rate, blood pressure, steps and workouts beside the journal and last night's sleep on
the page, drawn against the person's own usual range, with noticing in recaps that cites both
sides and no medical claims. Both pages end with decisions for the owner. Mockups for Android,
iPhone and the computer, light and dark, are in `docs/design/mockups/phone-apps/` and
`docs/design/mockups/phone-health/`.
