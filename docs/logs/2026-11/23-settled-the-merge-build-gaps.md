[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md#when-the-computer-already-holds-a-memory) adds "Also
kept on {name}" to a thing kept in both places, its own Stop confirmation for bringing things in,
which stops the computer too, and two Remove confirmations for what was put aside: removing a copy
after bringing things in, and deleting for good after using the computer's memory. A phone removed
while its things arrive brings nothing in; one removed while they are added keeps its own memory,
and the computer says so.
