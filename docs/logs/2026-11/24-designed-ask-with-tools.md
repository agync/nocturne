[Ask with tools](/design/ask-with-tools.md) is new. A phone with an online mind answered "What's
on tomorrow?" with "Nothing you've kept answers that." because Ask finds only by the question's
words and no mind knew the date. The proposal gives every answer today's date and time zone, adds a
date step that turns "tomorrow" or "last Tuesday" into days and fetches their calendar and journal
lines, and lets the mind ask for read-only look-ups when the words are not enough, each returning
kept lines with their receipts. It covers the fallback for a mind that cannot look things up, the
line shown while it looks, What was sent in parts, cost, parity on the phone and the computer,
seven worked examples and five open questions, with mockups.
