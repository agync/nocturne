[Ask with tools](/design/ask-with-tools.md) is new. A phone with an online mind answered "What's
on tomorrow?" with "Nothing you've kept answers that." because Ask finds only by the question's
words and no mind knew the date. The design gives every answer today's date and time zone, adds a
date step that turns "tomorrow" or "last Tuesday" into days and fetches their calendar, journal and
health lines, and lets the mind ask for read-only look-ups when the words are not enough, each
returning kept lines with their receipts. It covers the fallback for a mind that cannot look things
up, the line shown while it looks, What was sent in parts, cost, parity on the phone and the
computer, eight worked examples, and mockups.

The owner settled its five questions on 2026-11-24: health readings are in Ask on every mind the
person chose, in the health wording; the computer's own mind looks things up only on a size that
passes a new look-up check; each request counts towards the monthly limit, and setup says a
question can count more than once; the line while looking names the place; and check-ins are in
the journal look-up, sent like other journal lines. The pages these change are listed on the design
and change with the build.
