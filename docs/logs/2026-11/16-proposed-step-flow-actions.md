[Keeping the next step in view](/design/step-flow-actions.md) was proposed with three layouts and is
now approved: one bar pinned to the bottom of every step flow, the main button first on the left
everywhere including first-run setup, and the keep or ask bar hidden on narrow windows during a flow.
Step 1 of Online answers and of Away from home get a few short true lines and one expander, with the
full detail shown again before anything is turned on. None of it is in the interface yet.
