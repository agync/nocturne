title: "Kept a forget to removals while backup is off."
description: "A forget for good while backup is off and the backup kept removes the thing from the kept nights as when it is on, and sends nothing kept since."
tags: [log, update]
kind: Changed
status: stable
---

Forgetting something for good, or deleting a thing, a day or a recap, while backup is off and the
backup kept reaches the kept nights exactly as when backup is on: a new index without it, and every
older index that lists it removed with its pieces. The new index holds the newest kept night without
what was forgotten, dated as that night, so nothing kept or changed since goes to the account. See
[Turn off](/design/phone-backup.md#turn-off).
