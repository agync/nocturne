[Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md) shows "Getting your memory ready to move…" on the
phone and "{name} is getting its memory ready…" on the computer while the phone makes the copy that
will travel, each with a moving bar that shows no amount and "Stop". The amount bar and "Arriving
from {name}" begin once sending starts.
