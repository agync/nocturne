[Phone and computer as equal starting points](/design/phone-parity.md) now shows the final decision
on every question, with five corrections from the owner. A household computer lends its mind only to
phones its owner invites and keeps nothing of them beyond the name and key that let them in. A
memory moves to another machine only when its person pairs to make it their home, so [A household
computer](/design/household-server.md) separates borrowing the mind from making a household
computer your home, and [Moving your memory](/design/moving-memory.md) says so. The phases follow
the ladder: a phone alone, then online, then the household. Nothing is held back from an online mind
unless it is marked private.
