Invariant 2 in AGENTS.md keeps the backup clause for a phone that is its own home and adds what may
still go once the person turns backup off or deletes everything: only the removals they asked for
in their own iCloud or Google Drive, a forget for good or deleting the backup, with the same sign-in
and listing. [0019](/adr/0019-a-locked-copy-online.md#invariant-2) and
[0018](/adr/0018-a-phone-as-its-own-home.md#proposed-contract-changes) quote the same words.
