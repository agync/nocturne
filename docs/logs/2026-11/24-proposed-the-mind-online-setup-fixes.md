[Setting up Nocturne's mind online](/design/mind-online-setup.md) is new and proposes two fixes.
On the phone, step 4's "Turn on" sat at the end of a long scroll, so people saw only the back
arrow; the computer's pinned bar now applies to every phone screen that ends in a bar, at phone
sizes, at 200 per cent text and around the keyboard. On the details step, "Model name" stays a field
anyone can type in, and gains "Choose from {host}'s list…", which takes the check's one look at the
service's list when asked and shows the names to search and choose, with plain words for an empty
list, no list and a list that could not be fetched. Online answers' Connect on the computer is the
same form and gets the same list. Mockups and before and after renders are in
`docs/design/mockups/mind-online-setup/`; four questions wait for the owner.
