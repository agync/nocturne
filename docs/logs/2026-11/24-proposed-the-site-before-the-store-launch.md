[The site before the store launch](/design/site-refresh.md) is new. It checks every claim on the
landing and install pages against the code, and proposes what changes once Nocturne is in Google
Play and the App Store: a band for the phone apps with the store badges; Privacy, the journal and
Engine reworded so they hold for a phone, with "Two extras" and "Never anything marked private"
retired; Android and iPhone on the install page and a visitor message for phones; the Mac's first
open in two steps once it is signed; and a new support page. Three lines are untrue today and can be
fixed at once: the Flatpak does start with the computer, the first page comes at the chosen time,
and the AI chip does more than read voice notes. The privacy policy's own words are a separate
design; this page fixes only its address, `/privacy`, and where it is linked from. Each part is
staged to go live with what it describes, and the before and after renders are in
`docs/design/mockups/site-refresh/`.
