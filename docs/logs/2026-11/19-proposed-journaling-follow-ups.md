[Journaling follow-ups](/design/journaling-follow-ups.md) designs what building the
[journal](/design/journaling.md) left open. "Delete this day" groups what was kept on the day apart
from what other days share, gives each row a kind icon, a time and a readable snippet, folds
earlier versions into one row, and counts what goes. A check-in's own page drops the actions that
do not apply to it. Deleting a recap, and now a reflection, is confirmed and says the originals
stay. The page question from the journal has a rule built from stored facts and fixed templates
around the person's own sentence. Recaps, which are put together with no writing, are proposed for
every computer, awaiting the owner's approval. Ten strings the build added are settled in a copy
table. The journaling page is updated to match, and mockups in light and dark are in
`docs/design/mockups/journaling/`.
