[0018 A phone as its own home](/adr/0018-a-phone-as-its-own-home.md) proposes the phone keeping its
own memory with the computer's engine, one home at a time, moved only by pairing, and holds the
proposed wording for the contract, including Local only in full. [0019 A locked copy
online](/adr/0019-a-locked-copy-online.md), [0020 An online mind on a
phone](/adr/0020-an-online-mind-on-a-phone.md) and [0021 A household mind for invited
phones](/adr/0021-a-household-mind-for-invited-phones.md) each add one clause. All four await the
owner.
