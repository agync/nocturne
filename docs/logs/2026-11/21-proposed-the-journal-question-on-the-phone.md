[The journal question on the phone](/design/phone-journal-question.md) shows the morning page's
question from the journal on a paired phone's page, after the page's lines, with Fine, Not great
and Not yet and the optional line, all in the computer's words. The receipt opens the journal day
the block was kept on. An answer from either device shows on the other, an answer pressed while
the computer is away waits on the phone like a keep, and a retried answer is kept once. The phone's
journal gains the answer row the computer already draws. [Journaling](/design/journaling.md) and
[Journaling follow-ups](/design/journaling-follow-ups.md) point to it, and phone mockups in light
and dark are in `docs/design/mockups/journaling/`.
