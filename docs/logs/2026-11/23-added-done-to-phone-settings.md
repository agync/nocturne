[Nocturne on Android and iPhone](/design/phone-apps.md#9-this-phone) and [Health from the
phone](/design/phone-health.md#on-the-phone) end the calendar and Health settings with "Done", the
main button, which goes back; choices still save as they are made. On a phone alone, [Nocturne on a
phone of its own](/design/phone-first.md#reused-screens-worded-for-a-phone-alone) moves "Delete
what was kept…" into a quiet "Kept so far" group above "Done", so a destructive link is never the
last thing on the screen. Calendars that share a name show their account as a second line. The
affected mockups are re-rendered.
