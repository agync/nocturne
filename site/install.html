Free and open source, for Mac, Windows and Linux. No account, nothing to sign in to.

Choose your computer below. Each link gives the latest version.

Mac

Apple chip, M1 or later · macOS 26 or later

For this computer

Download for Mac

Nocturne-macos-arm64.dmg

Macs with an Intel chip are not supported. To see which chip yours has, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac.

The first time you open it

  1. Open the downloaded file, then drag Nocturne into Applications.
  2. Open Nocturne from Applications. Your Mac says Nocturne Not Opened, because it could not check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the line saying Nocturne was blocked and click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.

You only do this once. Apple charges a yearly fee to sign an app so it opens without asking; Nocturne is free and open source, and this release is not signed yet.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

For this computer

Download for Windows

Nocturne-windows-x64-setup.exe

Installs for you alone and needs no administrator password. Computers managed by an organisation can use the .msi installer instead.

The first time you open it

  1. Your browser may warn that the file is not commonly downloaded. In Edge, open the … menu beside the file, choose Keep, then Show more, then Keep anyway.
  2. Open the installer. Windows shows Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

You only do this once. Microsoft charges a yearly fee to sign an app so it opens without asking; Nocturne is free and open source, and this release is not signed yet.

Linux

64-bit computer with an Intel, AMD or Arm chip

For this computer

  • From your software centre

    The choice for most people, on any Linux desktop. Your software centre keeps Nocturne up to date. Starting with the computer is not available this way yet.

    Get the Flatpak
    Or install it from a terminal 
    flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists nocturne https://flatpak.nocturne.is/nocturne.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user nocturne is.nocturne.Nocturne

  • Ubuntu, Linux Mint or Debian

    Open the file with your software centre, and Nocturne appears with your other apps.

    .deb for Intel or AMD .deb for Arm

  • Fedora, Red Hat or openSUSE

    Open the file with your software centre, and Nocturne appears with your other apps.

    .rpm for Intel or AMD .rpm for Arm

  • Try it without installing

    One file that runs on most desktops. In its properties, allow it to run as a program, then open it.

    AppImage for Intel or AMD AppImage for Arm

Most Linux computers have an Intel or AMD chip. To check, run uname -m in a terminal: x86_64 means Intel or AMD, aarch64 means Arm. The Flatpak picks the right one by itself. The Arm downloads are new and have not yet been tried on an Arm desktop by a person.

Linux shows no warning the first time you open Nocturne.

Other computers

To make sure a download is the one we published, compare it with the fingerprints in SHA256SUMS. The install guide shows how, explains each package and how to take Nocturne off again. Earlier versions are on the releases page.

What your computer needs.

At least 8 GB of memory. The first time it opens, Nocturne looks at the computer and picks the largest setup it carries comfortably; there is nothing for you to choose. Every setup does everything; smaller ones answer more simply and tidy more slowly.

Large

64 GB memory · 28 GB space

Clearer answers to harder questions.

Standard

32 GB memory · 11 GB space

Many recent laptops.

Small

16 GB memory · 7 GB space

Same page. The overnight tidy takes longer.

Tiny

8 GB memory · 5.2 GB space

Simpler answers that may miss details.

After you install.

Nocturne opens a short setup. You can keep things straight away.

  1. When it opens

    It looks at the computer

    It tells you which setup it will use. You can always pick a smaller one, which uses less memory, space and battery.

  2. Once, when you agree

    It downloads what it needs

    It asks first, then downloads what this computer can run: between about 3 GB and 26 GB. This is the only time Nocturne needs the internet, and it carries on by itself if the connection drops.

  3. The next morning

    Your first page

    From then on, Nocturne works with no internet at all.
