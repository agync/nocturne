How it works

Keep it now. Read it once.

Keep · any time

Keep anything, file nothing.

Press Ctrl Shift Space, then type, talk, paste or drop a photo or a PDF. No folders, no tags. Nocturne says “Kept.” and you get back to your day. If it cannot read something, it tells you what to do.

Ask · any time

Answers with receipts.

Ask in plain words. Nocturne answers only from what you kept, with the originals underneath so you can check. When it does not know, it says so.

The morning page · 07:00

Wake to what needs you.

Overnight, while the computer is plugged in and idle, Nocturne reads what you kept and notices what changed. In the morning you get a short list to read, hear or print. When two things disagree, it asks instead of guessing.

What it promises

Your life stays on your computer.

Out of the box, Nocturne goes online only for downloads you agree to: its mind at setup and any update you allow. Privacy in the app lists each one with the day and the size.

Nothing to sign in to
No account, no analytics, no crash reports, no cloud copy.
Checked, not just claimed
Before a release, Nocturne is run with its network cut off from everything but this computer, and any attempt to reach further fails the check.
It forgets on purpose
Things you no longer use fade, and the page says when one is let go. Bring any back; forgetting for good always asks first.
One quiet note a day
When the page is ready, one note says so and how many things need you. Never what you kept.
Two extras, off until you ask
Away from home lets your own phone ask, keep and read your page, sealed so the company in between cannot read it. Online answers sends a question, and the few remembered sentences it needs, to one service you choose. Never anything marked private.

The engine

Built on

Nocturne’s thinking runs on Embeddable Lemonade, the portable build of Lemonade, the open-source local AI server. Nocturne ships its own copy, Lemonade {{LEMONADE_VERSION}}, that only this computer can reach.

Lemonade is free under the Apache 2.0 licence and built by its community, with optimisations by AMD engineers. Nocturne would not exist without it.

Requirements

It fits the computer you have.

Macs with an Apple chip on macOS 26 or later, 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, and 64-bit Linux on Intel or AMD. At setup Nocturne offers the largest setup your computer carries comfortably. Every setup does everything; smaller ones answer more simply and tidy more slowly.

Large

60 GB+ memory · 28 GB space

Clearer answers to harder questions.

Standard

28 GB+ memory · 11 GB space

Many recent laptops with 32 GB.

Small

14 GB+ memory · 7 GB space

Same page. The overnight tidy takes longer.

Tiny

7 GB+ memory · 5.2 GB space

Made for 8 GB computers. Simpler answers that may miss details.

The one-off download at setup is between about 3 GB and 26 GB, depending on the setup. After that, Nocturne works with no internet at all.
